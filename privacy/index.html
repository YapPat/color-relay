Color Relay: Privacy and Support

Color Relay: Block Puzzle Game. Last updated November 2, 2026.

In short

Color Relay is a relaxing block puzzle game. It has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or other personal details. Your scores and settings stay on your device. The game is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google's ad software collects some data, as explained below.

Saved on your device

The game saves your settings, best scores, level, Daily progress and any unfinished game on your device only, so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device, and we can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Color Relay shows a full-screen ad at natural breaks, no more than once every few minutes, and an optional video you can choose to watch for a second chance in Classic mode.

To show and measure ads, Google's ad software on your device may collect and use:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID on Android or the IDFA on iPhone (only if you allow tracking),
  • your IP address and the approximate location that comes from it,
  • information about the ads you see and tap, and how you interact with the app,
  • basic app and device information, such as device model and operating system,
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information.

Google handles this data under its privacy policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

We don't receive or store this data. We only see combined, anonymous reports from AdMob, such as how many ads were shown.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, the app only uses your advertising identifier if you tap Allow when asked. You can change this in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

If you decline, you still see ads, but they aren't personalized.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it saved. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing else for us to delete. For data Google collected to show ads, use Google's ad controls, reset your advertising identifier in your device's settings, or contact us and we'll help.

Purchases

There are no in-app purchases. Everything in the game is free and unlocks by playing.

Sharing

If you tap Share after a game, your device's share screen opens with a short message containing your score and the name of the game. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

Children

Color Relay is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
